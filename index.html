Hex Mosaic: Copy & Color

Hex Mosaic is a relaxing picture puzzle: look at the mosaic on the left, pick a color and tap the hexagon cells on the right to copy it, then press CHECK.

How to play

  • Tap a color at the bottom (or on the right side on iPhone), then tap or drag over cells to paint them.
  • Use the eraser to clear a cell.
  • Press CHECK when you are done. Wrong cells blink red; if some cells are still empty, they blink yellow.
  • Finish a picture without wrong cells to earn 3 stars and unlock the next picture.

Questions

  • Where is my progress saved? On your device. Uninstalling the App deletes it.
  • How do I turn the sound off? Tap the music note button in the top-left corner.
  • How do I start a picture again? Tap the circular arrow button twice.
